How we work

From dolphin encounters to snorkeling adventures, we make sure every trip is safe, fun and unforgettable. Boats are checked and cleaned before each departure, and every group gets a short briefing before you leave the marina.

Booking is handled by the same people who will be on the boat with you, so questions about dates, meeting points or extras get answered by someone who knows the answer.
